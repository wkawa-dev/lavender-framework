## What we store

When you send the contact form we store your name, email, optional company, and message in our database so we can reply.

We do not sell or share this data. Replace this page with your own notice before launch.

## Cookies

The site sets a session cookie and a CSRF cookie. Both are essential and expire when you close the browser or after two hours.
